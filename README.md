<h1>ESP32-S3-Box-3 Projects &amp; Experiments</h1> <p>Welcome! 👋 This is where I share the projects, experiments, ideas, and customizations I create for my ESP32-S3-Box-3.</p> <p>Right now, the ESP32-S3-Box-3 is my main device and the focus of the projects you'll find here. In the future, I hope to add more ESP32 devices and expand into even more projects, experiments, and connected ideas.</p> <h2>What I Do</h2> <p>I take inspiration from projects, ideas, and code created by others in the ESP32 community, along with the help of AI, and combine, modify, and adapt them to create something that works the way I want it to on my ESP32-S3-Box-3.</p> <p>This is my place to experiment, learn, and bring different ideas together into a single setup that fits my needs.</p> <h2>Current Features</h2> <p>So far, my ESP32-S3-Box-3 can do things like:</p> <ul> <li>📞 <strong>VoIP</strong> — Make and manage VoIP calls, including placing calls to the real world through a VoIP provider and Incredible PBX.</li> <li>💡 <strong>Smart Lights</strong> — Control my lights.</li> <li>🚪 <strong>Front Door Lock</strong> — Control my front door lock.</li> <li>📺 <strong>TV Control</strong> — Use IR to control my TV.</li> <li>🌀 <strong>Fan Control</strong> — Control IR-enabled fans.</li> <li>❄️ <strong>AC Control</strong> — Control my air conditioner through IR.</li> <li>🔊 <strong>Soundbar Control</strong> — Control my soundbar using IR.</li> <li>🔐 <strong>PIN Protection</strong> — Protect settings and controls with a PIN code.</li> <li>🚪🔒 <strong>Lock Protection</strong> — Enable or disable front-door-lock functionality whenever I want.</li> </ul> <h2>The Goal</h2> <p>This project is constantly evolving. I'll continue adding new ideas, features, experiments, and improvements as I come up with them.</p> <p>Eventually, I'd like to expand beyond the ESP32-S3-Box-3 and incorporate additional ESP32 devices to create even more connected projects.</p> <p>This is essentially my personal ESP32 playground — a place where I can take ideas from the community, experiment with them, combine different projects, and turn them into something that works the way I want.</p> <p>🚀 <strong>Build.
